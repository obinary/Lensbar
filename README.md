# Lensbar

**Lensbar** is a fast screen recorder and screenshot tool for Windows and Mac, by **Obinary**.

Press **Ctrl+Alt+R** (Windows) or **⌃⌥R** (Mac) and the capture bar slides out from the edge of your screen.

## Download

**[⬇ Download for Windows](https://github.com/obinary/lensbar/releases/latest/download/Lensbar-Setup.exe)** (Windows 10 and 11)

**[⬇ Download for Mac](https://github.com/obinary/lensbar/releases/latest/download/Lensbar.dmg)** (macOS 14 Sonoma or later; Apple silicon and Intel)

Older versions are on the [Releases](https://github.com/obinary/lensbar/releases) page.

**Windows:** run `Lensbar-Setup.exe` and follow the steps. To remove Lensbar later, use **Windows Settings → Apps**.

**Mac:** open `Lensbar.dmg` and drag Lensbar into **Applications**. Lensbar isn't from the App Store yet, so the first time:
1. Open Lensbar. If macOS says it can't check it, go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
2. The first time you capture, allow **Screen & System Audio Recording** for Lensbar, then quit and reopen it.

The Mac shortcut is **⌃⌥R** (Control + Option + R), and Lensbar lives in the menu bar.

## Browser extension

**Lensbar for browsers** puts Lensbar under a toolbar button in Chrome, Edge, Opera, Brave and Firefox:
- **Screenshots:** the visible part, a selected area, or the **whole page from top to bottom**.
- **Recordings:** a tab, an area of it, or the whole browser window, with tab sound and microphone.

Store links will appear here once it's approved in each store. Meanwhile the packages are on the [browser extension release](https://github.com/obinary/lensbar/releases/tag/ext-v0.1). Privacy: [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

## What it does

- **Record or screenshot**, using one of five modes: fixed size, select an area, draw any shape, full screen, or a single window.
- **Sound:** record desktop sound and your microphone, each with its own on/off switch, which you can also flip while recording.
- **Small while recording:** the controls shrink into a small notch at the screen edge that shows the timer. Hover over it to pause, stop or discard.
- **Pin:** pin the bar to keep it handy. It tucks into a small tab on the edge and opens again when you hover over it.
- **Pick an edge:** place the bar on the top, left or right edge of your screen.
- **Where files go:** videos are saved as MP4 in `Videos\Lensbar` (Mac: `Movies/Lensbar`), and screenshots as PNG in `Pictures\Lensbar`.

## Updates

Lensbar updates itself from this page. Every new version published here is picked up automatically, or with **Settings → Check for updates**. You can switch automatic installs off in Settings.
