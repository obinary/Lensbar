# Lensbar privacy policy

_Last updated: 21 November 2026_

Lensbar (the Windows app, the Mac app and the browser extension) is made by Obinary.

**Lensbar doesn't collect, store or share any personal data.**

- **Screenshots and recordings stay on your device.** They're saved to the folder you choose (by default Downloads/Lensbar, or Videos/Pictures › Lensbar in the desktop apps). Lensbar never uploads them anywhere.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** Lensbar has no servers that receive your data.
- **Settings stay on your device.** Your preferences (for example the microphone switch or the image format) are kept in your browser's or computer's local storage.
- **Microphone and screen access** are used only while you are recording, and only after you start the recording yourself.
- **Clipboard:** when "Copy to clipboard" is on, Lensbar puts your screenshot on your clipboard so you can paste it.
- **Update checks** (desktop apps only): the apps ask GitHub whether a newer version exists. This is a plain download request that contains no personal information.

The browser extension asks for these permissions, and uses them only when you use Lensbar:
`activeTab` and `scripting` (show the capture bar on the page you're on), `tabCapture` and `offscreen` (record the tab), `downloads` (save files), `storage` (remember settings).

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/obinary/lensbar/issues
