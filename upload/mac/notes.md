First **macOS** version of Lensbar.

- Same capture bar, five capture modes, recording notch, pin and settings as on Windows.
- Shortcut: ⌃⌥R. Lensbar lives in the menu bar.
- Needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later. Works on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- First launch: if macOS blocks it, use System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway.
