First version of **Lensbar for browsers**.

- Screenshots: visible part, an area, or the full page top to bottom.
- Recordings: this tab, an area of it, or a screen or window, with tab sound and microphone.

`lensbar-chromium.zip` is for Chrome, Edge, Opera and Brave. `lensbar-firefox.zip` is for Firefox 140+.
These packages are for the extension stores (and developers). Normal installs will come from the stores.
